# Guidelines for the Learner

## How to get the best results

1. Write your own answer before asking for the expert answer.
2. Keep all mistakes in `memory/mistakes.md`; do not hide them.
3. Ask the AI to challenge you from multiple roles.
4. Treat each weekly workshop as a portfolio artifact.
5. Do not deploy resources blindly. Review cost, region, SKU, and access settings first.

## Recommended weekly time

- Minimum: 5 sessions of 45 minutes plus one 2-hour workshop.
- Strong pace: 5 sessions of 60–90 minutes plus one 3-hour workshop.

## When the AI behaves badly

If the AI gives generic advice, say:

```text
Stop. Read system/memory_protocol.md, memory/progress.md, memory/mistakes.md, and the current training file. Continue based on context only.
```
