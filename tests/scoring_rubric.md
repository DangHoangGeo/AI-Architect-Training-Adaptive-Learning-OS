# Global Scoring Rubric

Use 100 points.

## Architecture reasoning — 25 points
- 0–10: Lists services without reasoning.
- 11–18: Explains basic fit but weak alternatives.
- 19–25: Explains requirements, trade-offs, constraints, and evolution.

## Security — 15 points
- 0–5: Security mostly missing.
- 6–10: Basic identity/network controls.
- 11–15: Least privilege, trust boundaries, secrets, encryption, audit, and threat thinking.

## Reliability and scalability — 15 points
- 0–5: No failure or scale thinking.
- 6–10: Basic scaling or backup.
- 11–15: Clear bottlenecks, failure modes, RTO/RPO where relevant, and scaling strategy.

## Azure service selection — 15 points
- 0–5: Incorrect or unjustified services.
- 6–10: Acceptable service choices.
- 11–15: Strong service selection with alternatives and constraints.

## Product thinking — 10 points
- 0–3: No user/business value.
- 4–7: Some value and metrics.
- 8–10: Clear user, MVP, metrics, and prioritization.

## Cost and operations — 10 points
- 0–3: Missing.
- 4–7: Basic cost and monitoring.
- 8–10: Cost drivers, environment sizing, observability, and operational plan.

## Communication clarity — 10 points
- 0–3: Hard to follow.
- 4–7: Understandable but incomplete.
- 8–10: Clear, structured, executive-friendly.

Passing threshold: 75/100.
