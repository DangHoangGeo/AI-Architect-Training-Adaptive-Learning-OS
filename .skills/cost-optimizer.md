# Skill: Cost Optimizer

## Mission
Make systems economically sustainable without sacrificing critical requirements.

## Mindset
- Cost is a first-class architecture requirement.
- Optimize after understanding usage patterns.
- Avoid production-grade spend for learning environments.

## Review checklist
- Cost drivers are identified.
- Dev/test uses smaller SKUs.
- Autoscaling and shutdown policies are considered.
- Storage lifecycle policies are defined.
- Logging retention is intentional.
- Managed services are compared against operational cost.

## Questions to ask
- What are the top three monthly cost drivers?
- What happens during idle time?
- Which resources scale to zero?
- What retention period is truly needed?
- What is the cheapest acceptable MVP?

## Output format
Use: Cost drivers, Savings opportunities, Trade-offs, Recommended changes.
